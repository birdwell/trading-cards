2016 Football Card Sets, Checklists
By Ryan Cracknell
8
Share:

For the past few years, Panini has used the Prizm line as its primary chromium football line, at least outside of premium brands. 2016 Donruss Optic Football adds another one to the mix, one that might console those lamenting the loss of Topps Chrome (as a physical product) with Panini’s NFL exclusive.

Similar to how Topps Chrome drew primarily from the flagship Topps release each year, 2016 Donruss Optic closely mirrors many of the same elements as 2016 Donruss Football but with a chromium twist.

Each hobby box comes with one autograph and numerous inserts.

Base Set

The base set adopts the same general design as the main Donruss set. This includes subsets like Rated Rookies and their posed portraits from the Rookie Premiere. Every hobby pack averages either a Rated Rookies card or another rookie. Every 20-pack box has ten of each.

Following the chrome tradition, parallels come in a rainbow of colors. These include Holo, Aqua (/299), Orange (/199), Blue (/149), Red (/99), Carolina Blue (/50), Black (/25), Gold (/10), Green (/5) and the Superfractor-like Gold Vinyl (1/1).

Hobby boxes have eight parallels.

Autographs

2016 Donruss Optic Football autographs are a mix of on-card and stickers. Rated Rookie Autographs are signed versions of the main cards. This is where some of the hard-signed cards are found.

Other signed inserts featuring 2016 draft picks include Rookie Dual and Triple Autographs, and Rookie Patch Autographs.

The Legends Series Autographs checklist includes 15 retired players. Fans of the Game Autographs return with signatures from celebrities like Daisy Ridley.
